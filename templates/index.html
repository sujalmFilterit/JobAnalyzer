Features

Everything you need to stay safe

Six layers of AI-powered protection working together to catch scams before they catch you.

AI Scam Detection

Our ML model trained on thousands of real and fake job postings identifies fraudulent patterns with 98%+ accuracy.

Company Validation

Cross-references the company name against our verified corporate database using fuzzy matching to catch imposters.

Salary Analysis

Flags unrealistic salary promises that are a hallmark of job scams — too good to be true usually is.

Keyword Detection

Scans for suspicious phrases like "work from home", "no experience needed", and "wire transfer" that signal fraud.

Domain Verification

Checks if the company's email domain matches a legitimate business or is a free/disposable email provider.

Real-time Confidence

Every result comes with a probability score so you know exactly how confident the AI is in its verdict.

About

Built to protect job seekers

Fake Job Detector is an AI-powered platform built as a minor project to tackle the growing epidemic of fraudulent job postings. Every year, millions of job seekers lose money and personal data to scammers posing as legitimate employers.

Our model is trained on real-world data and uses natural language processing to identify the subtle linguistic patterns that separate genuine job postings from scams — so you can apply with confidence.
